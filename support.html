Before you write

Things that are probably already answered

How do I report a bug usefully?

Three things make almost any report actionable: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. If you can add the device and whether you were online at the time, that's usually enough to reproduce it.

Screenshots help more than descriptions of screenshots.

I've lost access to my TabiLog household

Households share one set of credentials, so the first thing to try is another family member who's still signed in. If nobody can get in, email the studio from an address connected to the household and we'll work out what can be done.

Be aware there is a deliberate limit here: households are isolated by design, and that isolation is not something the studio can casually reach around.

Can I get a copy of everything my household has stored?

Yes. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF from inside the app at any time, for one person or for everyone. You don't need to ask anyone for that, and it isn't behind a paid tier.

Can I delete my household entirely?

Yes. Account Settings has a self-service Delete Account option. It asks for your password, and it permanently wipes the household and everything in it. Export first if you want to keep anything.

When is TabiPace or TabiPlanner coming out?

TabiPace v0.1 is built and signed, and is waiting on its Google Play listing. TabiPlanner is in development and needs to be usable by 1 September 2026.

Both get announced on the updates page when there's something real to say. Nothing gets a launch date here before it deserves one.

Will you add feature X?

Maybe. Suggestions genuinely do shape these apps. But each one is deliberately narrow, and quite a few good ideas get declined because they belong in a different app, or in no app at all. If yours is declined you'll get a reason rather than silence.

Still stuck?

Write anyway. A question that's already been answered somewhere is still worth asking if you couldn't find the answer. That's usually a sign the answer is in the wrong place.
