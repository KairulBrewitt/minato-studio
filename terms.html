Your account and your household

A TabiLog household shares one set of credentials among the people you invite. That's a deliberate design choice, and it means everyone in your household can see and change everything in it. Choose who you share credentials with accordingly, and keep them to yourself otherwise.

Creating a household requires an invite code. Please don't circulate it beyond the people it was meant for.

Your data, and keeping your own copies

Your data is yours. You can export it at any time, and delete all of it at any time, without asking anyone.

Please keep your own backups of anything you'd be upset to lose. This is a small studio without a support roster, and while the apps are built to be durable, no promise of continuous availability or guaranteed recovery is being made. Export is right there for exactly this reason.

No professional advice

None of these apps provide financial, medical or travel advice.

  • TabiLog records what you tell it about money. It is not financial advice and not an accounting system of record.
  • TabiPace models physical pacing based on limits you enter. It is not medical advice, it is not a clinical tool, and it does not know anything about anyone's actual health. Where it handles medication it only shifts the times you entered into destination local time — it will never suggest a dose or a change. Those questions are for a doctor or pharmacist.
  • TabiPlanner records the trip you planned. It does not verify opening hours, prices, bookings or travel conditions.

Decisions about your family's safety, health and money remain yours.
